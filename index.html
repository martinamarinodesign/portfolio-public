ABOUT ME

I came to design through fine arts and new media — which really just means I spent years looking at things before anyone asked me to turn them into screens. That habit never left.

Now I work across UX, UI, and generative AI, and I care about all three being done well, not just done fast. I've designed banking and public admin interfaces at Accenture Song, edited medical video courses in Berlin, and co-wrote a jingle that ended up on national TV. I use AI tools daily and I have zero patience for slop.

Off-screen: theatre, acoustic guitar, kung fu. Best place on earth: Berlin in the summer.
